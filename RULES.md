# Hack or Defend — Game Rules

---

## What is Hack or Defend?

Hack or Defend is a card game about cybersecurity. Players read a cyber attack scenario and try to find the correct defense. The player with the most correct answers at the end wins.

The game can be played with the digital app (available online) which shows the cards and manages the scores automatically.

---

## Players and Setup

- **Number of players:** 2 to 4
- **Duration:** around 15 to 30 minutes
- **What you need:** a device (phone, tablet or computer) with the app open, or the printed cards

Before starting, players choose:
1. The **number of rounds** they want to play (between 5 and 15)
2. The **difficulty level** (see below)
3. Each player enters their **name**

---

## Difficulty Levels

| Level | Timer | Hints |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Beginner | No timer | A hint is shown on the card |
| 🟡 Intermediate | No timer | No hints |
| 🔴 Expert | 20 seconds | No hints |

Choose the level before the game starts. You cannot change it during the game.

---

## How to Play

### Step 1 — Read the card
A new attack card appears on the screen (or is picked from the deck). All players read the scenario together.

The card shows:
- The **name** of the attack (ex: "Phishing")
- A **scenario**: a short story that describes the attack in a real situation
- The **category** of the attack (ex: Social Engineering, Web Attack...)
- A **hint** if you are playing in Beginner mode

### Step 2 — Find the defense
Each player thinks about which defense is the correct answer. Players can discuss together or keep their answer secret — it depends on what the group decides before the game.

In **Expert mode**, players have only **20 seconds** to give their answer. After 20 seconds, the answer is automatically revealed.

### Step 3 — Reveal the answer
One player clicks "Reveal Answer" (or flips the card if playing with printed cards).

The correct defense appears with a short explanation of why it works.

### Step 4 — Give points
Each player who had the correct answer gets **+1 point**. In the app, just tap the name of the players who were right.

### Step 5 — Next card
Click "Next Round" to go to the next card and repeat from Step 1.

---

## End of the Game

When all rounds are finished, the app shows the final scores and the ranking.

The player with the **most points wins**.

If two players have the same score, the winner is the one who gave more correct answers before the timer ran out (Expert mode). In other modes, it's a draw.

---

## Important Rules

- Players must give their answer **before** the reveal button is pressed. You cannot change your answer after.
- In Beginner mode, the hint is just here to help you think. It does not give the full answer.
- If a player is not sure, they can still try to guess. There are no points removed for wrong answers.
- One player can act as the **game master**: they read the cards out loud and press the reveal button. The game master can also play.

---

## Card Categories

The game has 15 cards in 7 categories:

- **Social Engineering** — attacks that manipulate people (ex: Phishing, Malicious USB Drop)
- **Web Attack** — attacks on websites or apps (ex: SQL Injection, XSS)
- **Authentication** — attacks on passwords and logins (ex: Brute Force, Credential Stuffing)
- **Malware** — malicious software (ex: Ransomware)
- **Network Attack** — attacks on internet connections (ex: DDoS, Man-in-the-Middle)
- **System** — attacks that use software vulnerabilities (ex: Zero-Day Exploit)
- **Internal Threat** — attacks from inside the company (ex: Insider Threat)
