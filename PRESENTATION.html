Why Cybersecurity?

  • 🎨 A designer needs to understand phishing and fake interfaces
  • 📊 A project manager needs to know about ransomware risks
  • 📣 A marketer who handles data needs to know what a data breach is
  • 📋 Cybersecurity is in more and more job offers in the digital sector

How the Game Works

1

An attack card is shown — players read the scenario together

2

Each player finds the correct defense and discusses their answer

3

The answer is revealed with an explanation of why it works

4

Players who got it right earn +1 point

5

Next card — most points at the end wins 🏆

Our Design Choices

📖 Scenarios, not definitions

A realistic story is easier to remember than a definition. Players can imagine the attack happening to them.

💡 Explanation after the answer

Players learn why the defense works, not just what it is. That's more useful in real life.

🗣 Card game, not a quiz

Players discuss together. When you explain your answer to someone else, you learn better.

🎚 3 difficulty levels

A class is never all beginners or all experts. 3 levels means everyone can play and progress.
